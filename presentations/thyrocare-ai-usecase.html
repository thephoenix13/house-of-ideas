Perfect Skills02 — The Problem
Where time was being lost

The organisation's knowledge existed. Getting to it did not scale.

—

SOPs and policies lived across multiple disconnected systems

—

Getting an operational data answer meant waiting for someone to build the report

—

Field staff and franchise partners depended on supervisors for routine queries

—

Compliance prep meant hunting through folders and old document versions

The knowledge existed. Nobody could reach it without a search job or a phone call.02
Perfect Skills03 — The Solution
What we built

RAG as the foundation. Enterprise intelligence as the goal.

Stage 1

Knowledge

Employees ask, get the right document, with a citation.

Stage 2

Intelligence

Ask a data question. Get the answer. No report request needed.

Stage 3

Action

AI initiates approved workflows, requests and reports.

Stage 1 works on its own. Stages 2 and 3 are where it becomes a platform.03
